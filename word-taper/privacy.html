Word Taper Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

The short version

  • Word Taper doesn't ask for your name, email, location or any account details.
  • Your game progress, hints and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are not personalised, and we ask Google to treat every user as a child under the relevant children's privacy laws.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • Questions? Email zoomiescreative@gmail.com.

Who we are

Word Taper is made by Zoomies Creative, an independent app studio based in Australia. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Zoomies Creative. This policy explains what information Word Taper handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to Word Taper on iOS and Android.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, Word Taper saves the following on your device only:

  • levels completed, scores and progress
  • how many hints you have
  • your settings, such as sound on or off

This information is not sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Word Taper is free to play and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads appear after a game is won, and you can choose to watch an ad to earn a free hint.

We have set up AdMob so that:

  • ads are not personalised and are not based on your interests or activity in other apps
  • every request is tagged as child-directed, which limits what Google collects and how it's used, in line with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws
  • ad content is limited to material suitable for all ages
  • the app does not access your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA)

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the app automatically sends some information to Google:

InformationWhat it's used for
IP address, which can indicate your general area (such as country or city, not your exact location)Showing ads suitable for your region, preventing fraud
Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, ad views and tapsDelivering and counting ads, preventing fraud
Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performanceKeeping ads working reliably
A device identifier specific to our apps (app set ID on Android)Counting ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, preventing fraud

This information goes directly to Google and is handled under Google's own policies. We don't receive information that identifies you personally. You can read more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

You can buy extra hints in the app. Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, under their terms and privacy policies. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only receives confirmation that a purchase was made so it can add your hints.

Children

Word Taper is designed for players of all ages, including children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no sign-up, chat, social features or ways to share personal details. As described above, ads are configured for a child audience and are not personalised.

If you're a parent or guardian and have questions about your child's use of Word Taper, please contact us at zoomiescreative@gmail.com. In-app purchases can be managed using the parental controls on your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools beyond the ad information described above.
  • We don't request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Where information is processed

Information collected by Google AdMob may be processed on Google's servers in the United States and other countries. Google applies its own safeguards to international transfers.

Keeping information secure

Information sent by the AdMob software is encrypted in transit. Game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the Australian Privacy Act, the EU and UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to how it is used. Because we don't hold personal information about you, most requests can be handled directly:

  • Delete your game data: uninstall Word Taper.
  • Ad data held by Google: see Google's privacy controls or contact Google.
  • Anything else: email us and we'll help or point you to the right place. We aim to reply within 30 days.

If you're in Australia and unhappy with how we've handled a privacy matter, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

If Word Taper changes how it handles information, for example by adding a new feature or service, we'll update this policy and the effective date above. For significant changes we'll also give notice in the app or on the store listing.
