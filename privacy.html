Privacy Policy

Last updated: 01/09/2026

Zoomies Creative builds independent mobile apps, including STOP ME. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use one of our apps.

What we collect

Our apps are built to work without collecting personal information. Any details you enter — like income or salary figures in STOP ME — stay on your device. We don't transmit this information to our servers, because we don't have servers that store it.

Advertising

Some of our apps show ads through Google AdMob to keep them free to use. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, in line with its own privacy practices. You can read Google's policy directly:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from AdMob

On iOS, you'll see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt where relevant, and you can decline tracking at any time in your device settings.

Your choices

Because we don't hold your data, there's nothing for us to delete or export on request. If you'd like to limit ad personalisation, you can do so in your device's privacy settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and adjust the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or any of our apps: zoomiescreative@gmail.com
