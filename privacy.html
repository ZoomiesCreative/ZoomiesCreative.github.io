Word Taper Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

The short version

  • Word Taper doesn't ask for your name, email, location or any account details.
  • Your game progress, hints and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show personalised ads, but only where you've allowed it, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • Questions? Email zoomiescreative@gmail.com.

Who we are

Word Taper is made by Zoomies Creative, an independent app studio based in Australia. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Zoomies Creative. This policy explains what information Word Taper handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to Word Taper on iOS and Android.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, Word Taper saves the following on your device only:

  • levels completed, scores and progress
  • how many hints you have
  • your settings, such as sound on or off

This information is not sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Word Taper is free to play and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. A banner ad appears at the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad may appear after a game, and you can choose to watch an ad to earn a free hint.

To deliver and measure ads, personalise them where permitted, and prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the app sends some information to Google:

InformationWhat it's used for
Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking)Showing personalised ads, counting ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, preventing fraud
IP address, which can indicate your general area (such as country or city, not your exact location)Showing ads suitable for your region, preventing fraud
Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, ad views and tapsDelivering, measuring and personalising ads, preventing fraud
Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, crashes and performanceKeeping ads working reliably

This information goes directly to Google and is handled under Google's own policies. We don't receive information that identifies you personally. You can read more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks whether it may track you before your advertising ID is used. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalised using your IDFA. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact menu varies by device).
  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: the app shows Google's consent form when you first open it, so you can choose whether your information is used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings on the app's home screen.
  • You can also manage ad personalisation at Google's My Ad Center.

In-app purchases

You can buy extra hints in the app. Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, under their terms and privacy policies. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only receives confirmation that a purchase was made so it can add your hints.

Children

Word Taper is a word puzzle for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no sign-up, chat, social features or ways to share personal details.

If you're a parent or guardian and have questions about your child's use of Word Taper, please contact us at zoomiescreative@gmail.com. In-app purchases can be managed using the parental controls on your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools beyond the ad information described above.
  • We don't request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Where information is processed

Information collected by Google AdMob may be processed on Google's servers in the United States and other countries. Google applies its own safeguards to international transfers.

Keeping information secure

Information sent by the AdMob software is encrypted in transit. Game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security.
