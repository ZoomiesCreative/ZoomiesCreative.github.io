STOP ME Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

The short version

  • STOP ME doesn't ask for your name, email, location or any account details.
  • Your watchlist, savings history and settings are saved only on your device — never sent anywhere.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. On iOS, we ask your permission before accessing your advertising identifier.
  • Questions? Email zoomiescreative@gmail.com.

Who we are

STOP ME is made by Zooomies Creative, an independent app studio based in Australia. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Zooomies Creative. This policy explains what information STOP ME handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to STOP ME on iOS.

Information stored on your device

To make the app work, STOP ME saves the following on your device only:

  • items you add to your watchlist
  • purchases you have chosen to decline
  • your savings history and goals
  • your settings, such as salary, currency and notification preferences

This information is not sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

STOP ME is free to use and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, we ask for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you allow it, AdMob may use it to show you more relevant ads. If you deny it, ads will still appear but will not be personalised based on your activity in other apps. You can change this permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the app automatically sends some information to Google:

InformationWhat it's used for
IP address, which can indicate your general area (such as country or city, not your exact location)Showing ads suitable for your region, preventing fraud
Device advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you grant ATT permissionShowing personalised ads, measuring ad performance
Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, ad views and tapsDelivering and counting ads, preventing fraud
Diagnostic information, such as app performance and crash reportsKeeping ads working reliably

This information goes directly to Google and is handled under Google's own policies. You can read more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

STOP ME is intended for adults managing their personal finances. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a privacy concern, please contact us at zoomiescreative@gmail.com.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools beyond the ad information described above.
  • We don't request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
  • We don't require you to create an account or provide any personal details to use the app.

Where information is processed

Information collected by Google AdMob may be processed on Google's servers in the United States and other countries. Google applies its own safeguards to international transfers. All data you enter into STOP ME stays on your device.

Keeping information secure

Information sent by the AdMob software is encrypted in transit. Your personal finance data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security features.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the Australian Privacy Act, the EU and UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws may give you rights over personal information. Because we don't hold personal information about you, most requests can be handled directly:

  • Delete your app data: uninstall STOP ME from your device.
  • Opt out of personalised ads (iOS): go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and disable tracking for STOP ME.
  • Ad data held by Google: see Google's privacy controls or contact Google directly.
  • Anything else: email us and we'll help or point you in the right direction. We aim to reply within 30 days.

If you're in Australia and unhappy with how we've handled a privacy matter, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

If STOP ME changes how it handles information, we'll update this policy and the date above. For significant changes we'll also give notice in the app or on the App Store listing.
